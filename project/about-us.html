About Samsu Swine Breed

The founder and CEO of Samsu Swine Breed, Samuel Msughter Iornder, has over 20 years of experience in the swine industry.
His passion for quality breeding has made Samsu Swine Breed a leading name in the business.

Our Values

  • Quality: We prioritize the quality of our swine breeds to ensure customer satisfaction.
  • Integrity: We conduct our business with honesty and transparency.
  • Innovation: We continuously seek new methods and technologies to improve our breeding practices.
  • Customer Focus: We listen to our customers and tailor our services to meet their needs.

History

Samsu Swine Breed was founded in 2000 with the mission to provide high-quality swine breeds for various purposes.
Over the years, we have grown to become a trusted name in the industry.

What We Do

At Samsu Swine Breed, we are committed to providing the highest quality swine breeds for our clients based on their specific needs, be it for meat production, breeding, or other purposes.
Our mission is to exceed expectations and build lasting relationships based on trust and excellence.
