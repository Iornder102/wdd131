SAMSU SWINE BREED siteplan

Proposed Name: SAMSU SWINE BREED

Reasoning: This site provides a platform to choose and obtain different breeds of pig for your desired purpose.

Optional Domain: Samsu Swine breed.org

Site Purpose

This site contains different breeds of Pig.
The purpose of this site is to provide customers with information on different breeds of Pig
and provide them with the opportunity to make choice of a breed or breeds based on their desired purpose.

Scenarios

  • Where can I find different breeds of Pigs based pork quality or for breeding purpose?
  • What are their prices?
  • How do I contact the management for inquiries or support?

Color Schema

The selected colors for this project demonstrate contrast and usability:

  • Primary Color: #1D3557 (Used for headers and navigation background)
  • Secondary Color: #457B9D (Used for accent borders, secondary headers, and links)
  • Accent Color: #E63946 (Used for call-to-action buttons and highlights)

Typography

Heading Font: 'Montserrat', sans-serif (Used for heading1, heading2, and heading3 elements)

Body Text Font: 'Roboto', sans-serif (Used for paragraphs, list items, and main body content)
